Audio assets
============

The bundled audio files come from OpenGameArt CC0 resources:

- Card Game sounds: https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds
- Dark Shrine Loop: https://opengameart.org/content/dark-shrine-loop
- Playing Card Sounds by BMacZero: https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds
- bomb_explosion_8bit by Luke.RUSTLTD: https://opengameart.org/content/bombexplosion8bit
- 25 CC0 bang / firework SFX by rubberduck: https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx
- Rocket Launch Sound Retro 8Bit by qzlocx: https://opengameart.org/content/rocket-launch-sound-retro-8bit

The filenames in this folder were normalized for app use.
Some files were converted to WAV, mixed down to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, or trimmed for short in-game feedback.
